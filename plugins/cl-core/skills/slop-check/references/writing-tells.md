# Writing tells and what to do instead

The house rules (`docs/house-rules.md`) hold the studio's taste; this list holds the generic tells the scanner looks for.

| Tell | Instead |
| --- | --- |
| Em dashes everywhere | A comma, a colon, a full stop, or a new sentence |
| "It's not X, it's Y" and "not just X" | Say what it is |
| Rule-of-three cadence in every headline and list | The real number of items, often two or four |
| Rhetorical questions as headings ("Why does this matter?") | A statement that answers it |
| "X: Y" headline pairs | One headline |
| Stock verbs and adjectives: unlock, elevate, empower, leverage, harness, seamless, effortless, robust, cutting-edge, transformative, bespoke, curated, meticulously crafted, intuitive, powerful | The specific verb or number; or delete the adjective |
| Stock openers: in today's world, in a world where, imagine, picture this, whether you're a, look no further, say goodbye to | Start with the subject |
| Stock connectors: furthermore, moreover, additionally, that said, at its core, it's worth noting | Drop them; sentence order carries the logic |
| Closers: in conclusion, hope this helps, the best part | End on the last fact |
| Exclamation marks | Full stops |
| Emoji in headings or bullets | None in product copy |
| Hedge stacks: can potentially help to | One verb |
| Restating the heading in the first sentence | Start with new information |
| Metaphor piles: journey, landscape, tapestry, realm | The literal word |
| Fake specificity: "10x", "99.9%", "thousands of teams" without a source | The real number with its source, or nothing |

## Procedure for a text

1. Scan, then read the flagged lines aloud; the tells are easier to hear than to see.
2. Replace with the specific word, number, or fact; where none exists, cut the sentence.
3. Run `text-audit` for anything a player or customer will read; its cold read catches what this list cannot.
